About

I'm a robotics engineer focused on reinforcement learning and sim-to-real transfer, taking walking policies from simulators like IsaacLab and MuJoCo and getting them running reliably on physical actuators. My Bachelor End Project at TU/e, a from-scratch bipedal robot (BDX-R), was graded 9/10 and walked in the real world by the end of it. I also document my builds on YouTube, where my project videos have picked up over 100K+ views.
